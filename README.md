## Publications

### Probing Cultural Awareness in LLMs: A Case Study of Cross-Culture Aesthetic Stylistics

**IJCAI 2026 Human-Centred AI Track** · Co-first Author  
arXiv: [2605.27296](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2605.27296)  
Code: [Culture-Awareness](https://github.com/TorkeMeo/Culture-Awareness/tree/movie/tmdb)

An LLM/NLP study evaluating how large language models understand cultural awareness and aesthetic stylistics in Hong Kong and Mainland China language contexts.

### Foresight Optimization for Strategic Reasoning in Large Language Models

**ACL 2026 Main Conference**  
arXiv: [2604.13592](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2604.13592)

This work studies how foresight-based optimization can improve strategic reasoning in large language models, with a focus on multi-agent interaction, long-term decision-making, and policy optimization.
